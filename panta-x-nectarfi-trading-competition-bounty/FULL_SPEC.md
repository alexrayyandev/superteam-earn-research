## Description

### Panta x NectarFi Trading Competition Bounty


### Overview

Panta Market is a permissionless prediction market built on Solana that lets anyone create markets, trade outcomes, and earn from market activity. Users can take YES or NO positions on real-world events and participate in markets created by the community.

For this competition, Panta Market is partnering with NectarFi to bring more users into the Panta ecosystem through a month-long trading competition supported by community-driven content and distribution.

Participants are encouraged to create and distribute original content around the competition, NectarFi, and Panta Market while participating in the competition and competing for top positions on the leaderboard.


### Bounty Objective

Your objective is to drive awareness, participation, and trading activity on Panta through the NectarFi trading competition.

Use your content, audience, and communities to:

Drive new users to Panta.


Encourage participation in the NectarFi trading competition.


Create original content that generates awareness and engagement.


Participate in trading activities on Panta.


Contribute to the Panta ecosystem through relevant market creation where possible.


The bounty runs for one month, giving participants time to create and distribute content consistently throughout the campaign.


### How to Participate

Sign up on Panta.


This week, trade at least $1 on Market 1 and Market 2 on Panta to qualify for the bounty.


Quote-tweet the official Panta announcement about the NectarFi competition and share it with your audience.


Create original content promoting the competition and encouraging people to participate on Panta.


Tag @PantaHQ, @Nectar_finance, and @SuperteamNG in your content and include #PantaMarkets.


Participants who create relevant and engaging markets on Panta during the competition may receive additional consideration during winner selection.


You can submit X posts, short threads, memes, videos, community posts, or other original content formats.

Leaderboard Rewards: Beyond the bounty, participants are encouraged to compete for additional rewards by trading on Panta or creating markets. The top five participants on the trading competition leaderboard will share a separate $1,000 prize pool.
